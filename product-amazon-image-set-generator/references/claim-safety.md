# Claim safety

Classify every commercial claim before it enters module copy:

- `VERIFIED`: directly supported by registered specifications, test evidence, or unambiguous product facts.
- `SUPPORTED_WITH_CAUTION`: supported enough for conservative wording but not an absolute performance statement.
- `NEEDS_PROOF`: requires supplier, specification, certification, or test evidence.
- `DO_NOT_USE`: contradicted, misleading, structurally false, competitor-derived, or outside the sellable product.

Visual appearance alone does not prove material grade, alloy, waterproofing, sweat resistance, hypoallergenic properties, durability, reinforcement, or certification. Do not upgrade generic leather to full-grain/top-grain, metallic appearance to stainless steel, or plausible performance to a tested claim.

Each claim record needs evidence paths and allowed wording. Only `VERIFIED` and appropriately qualified `SUPPORTED_WITH_CAUTION` claims may be used in an approved module. Keep prohibited or unsupported claims in the registry for audit rather than silently deleting the decision.

Keep supplied image copy in its original language and wording, independently of the language used for analysis. Do not default to US English or translate to Chinese because the conversation is Chinese. Preserve spelling, umlauts/accents, punctuation, numbers and units. Translate, correct or rewrite only when the current user has authorized that change; keep the source version for audit. If a claim cannot be used, retain and flag the original rather than silently replacing it with a softer invented sentence. Where new copy is actually requested, write concise natural wording in the requested/source language, mobile-readable and proportional to evidence. Avoid stacked superlatives and unsupported comparative language.
